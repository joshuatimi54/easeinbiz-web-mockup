5. Foundations — locked Round 2 ("Soft blend")

Soft physics: 10px radius on controls and cards, generous padding, shadows instead of borders. Inputs are soft filled fields (#F9FCFE on #E9EFF3). Pills stay fully round — buttons never do.

Radius: 10px controls/cards · 14px modals · 999px pills only

Spacing: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32

Shadows: --shadow-sm (controls) · --shadow-md (cards) · --shadow-lg (modals/popovers)

Card on shadow, no border

6. Buttons — locked Round 3

Secondary is a ocean tinted ghost. Destructive rests tinted, turns solid red on hover. Attention (orange) is rationed to upgrade/alert moments. Pressed = scale(.97). Hover any button below.

7. Forms — locked Round 4

Top labels (12px/500) over soft filled fields. Errors: red border, #FEF7F7 tint, 11px message under the field — no icons, no shouting. 16px stack gap between fields.

11. Modals — locked Round 8

Modals for everything. Sizes: sm 400px confirmations · md 560px forms · lg 760px detail views. 14px radius, shadow-lg, ink scrim at 45%.

12. Motion — locked Round 13 ("springy")

Default: --ease-spring at --dur-base (300ms) — things overshoot into place. Buttons lift 2px on hover, press to 94%. Cards lift 4px. Restraint rule: dense data (table rows, chips, validation) uses --ease-out at --dur-fast so scanning stays calm. Reduced motion respected globally.
